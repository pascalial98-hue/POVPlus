# AI 使用说明 / AI usage disclosure

POV+ Enhanced 的增强功能实现、原生接口分析、测试代码、构建脚本与说明文档使用了大量 AI 辅助。AI 根据维护者提出的具体功能要求、交互修正和游戏内反馈，编写和迭代了大部分新增代码。这不只是自动补全或翻译辅助。<br>
AI was used extensively for enhanced-feature implementation, native-interface analysis, tests, build scripts and documentation. It authored and iterated on most added code in response to the maintainer's specific requirements, interaction corrections and in-game feedback. Its involvement was not limited to autocomplete or translation.

维护者负责功能取舍、实际游戏操作及体验反馈；部分功能经过多轮游戏内验证。离线测试和代码检查也由 AI 协助编写、执行和分析。没有声称维护者已逐行人工审查全部代码，或能独立解释每一处恢复源码和原生 hook。具体已经验证和未验证的范围见 [VALIDATION.md](VALIDATION.md)。<br>
The maintainer selected features, operated the game and supplied usability feedback, including repeated in-game testing of some features. AI also helped write, run and interpret offline tests and code checks. We do not claim a complete line-by-line human audit or independent human understanding of every recovered-source detail and native hook. See [VALIDATION.md](VALIDATION.md) for tested and unverified scope.

插件图标、预览图的设计制作以及内嵌视点校准图使用了 AI 图像生成或编辑。预览以维护者提供的游戏截图为素材；校准图参考官方 FF14 角色形象重绘，并非官方插画或截图。具体来源见 [PROMOTIONAL_ART.txt](PROMOTIONAL_ART.txt)、[CALIBRATION_ART.md](CALIBRATION_ART.md) 和 [NOTICE.md](../NOTICE.md)。<br>
AI image generation or editing was used for the icon, promotional layout and embedded calibration diagram. Promotional art uses game screenshots supplied by the maintainer; the calibration diagram is a redraw based on official FFXIV character references, not official artwork or a game screenshot. Sources are documented in [PROMOTIONAL_ART.txt](PROMOTIONAL_ART.txt), [CALIBRATION_ART.md](CALIBRATION_ART.md) and [NOTICE.md](../NOTICE.md).

首次国服主库申请的准备、PR 正文和提交操作同样由 AI 按维护者明确授权协助完成。本说明本身也由 AI 整理。是否收录仍由国服维护者审核决定。<br>
Preparation, PR text and submission of the initial CN repository application were also assisted by AI with the maintainer's explicit authorization. This disclosure was drafted by AI as well. Acceptance remains subject to review by the CN repository maintainers.
